# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I book an appointment?**
A: Use the "Book an appointment" option on the kiosk screen, choose a department or doctor, and pick an available time slot.

**Q: I don't have an appointment, can I still be seen?**
A: Yes — use "Check in without an appointment" to get a queue token for the right department.

**Q: Can the robot tell me if I have a serious condition?**
A: No. BonBon can help you check in, book appointments, and find your way, but it cannot diagnose or give medical advice. A doctor will assess you.

**Q: What if I need help urgently?**
A: Press the red "Call staff" button on screen at any time — a staff member will be notified immediately.
