# Emergency Guidance

BonBon is not a medical device and cannot triage or diagnose emergencies.

If you or someone nearby has chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding,
sudden weakness/numbness on one side, or has lost consciousness: press the red
"Call staff" button immediately, or ask any staff member for help right away.
Do not wait for a queue token.

For life-threatening emergencies outside the hospital, call your local
emergency number.
