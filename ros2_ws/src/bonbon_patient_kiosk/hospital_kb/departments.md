# Departments

- **Cardiology** (Floor 2) — heart and cardiovascular care. Reachable by saying "take me to Cardiology" or "where is the heart clinic".
- **Orthopaedics** (Floor 3) — bones, joints, and muscles.
- **Paediatrics** (Floor 1) — care for infants, children, and adolescents.
- **General Practice** (Floor 1) — walk-in and general consultations, the default for anyone unsure which department they need.
